Chicken Tacos

Home

I thouroughly enjoy chicken. I could eat it every day. I also love black beans and avocado. That's why I love making these tacos.

This is a very simple recipe that will leave you stuffed for hours! It's also a "clean" meal for those of you on a diet. I don't have some life changing story about this recipe but it does make my life easier.

Ingredients

  • 35g Unsweetened Cocoa Powder
  • 150g Brown Sugar
  • 60g All purpose flour
  • 100g Semisweet Chocolate Chips
  • 1 Stick of Salted Butter
  • 2 eggs
  • The amount of vanilla extract your heart tells you

Steps

  1. Melt your butter in a pot
  2. Whisk your brown sugar into the butter and combine
  3. Melt your chocolate in the same pot
  4. Add your cocoa powder, eggs, and vanilla extract and mix
  5. Mix in flour
  6. Cook in your airfryer at 160c for 18 minutes. 15 if you like them slightly undercooked
  7. Enjoy!
