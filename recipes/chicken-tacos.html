Chicken Tacos

Home

I thouroughly enjoy chicken. I could eat it every day. I also love black beans and avocado. That's why I love making these tacos.

This is a very simple recipe that will leave you stuffed for hours! It's also a "clean" meal for those of you on a diet. I don't have some life changing story about this recipe but it does make my life easier.

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast
  • Half a can of black beans
  • 5-8 tortillas depending on how much you want to stuff them
  • 1 avocado
  • Sour cream or fat free plain yogurt
  • Monterey Jack or your favorite cheese
  • Half a can of corn (optional but if you need to make them more filling it helps)

Steps

  1. Boil chicken until done and then shred it
  2. Season the chicken with your favorite seasonings then butter a pan and brown the chicken
  3. Remove the chicken from the pan and heat up the beans
  4. Season and mash up the beans(add a little bit of water if needed) Go ahead and sprinkle the cheease to the beans
  5. Mix chicken and beans together
  6. Smash your avocado, add salt, pepper, and garlic powder to it for a quick guac
  7. Add your fillings to the taco and top with guac and sour cream/yogurt
  8. Enjoy!
