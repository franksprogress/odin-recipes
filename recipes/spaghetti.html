Spaghetti is such a good meal. So easy to make a lot of it and eat for days!!! And there's so many sauces to pick from. I personally love the Preggo Mushroom one.

I'm going to give you the one serving recipe for this but obviously you can do the math and easily expand this.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound of ground beef
  • 80g of whole wheat pasta(or regular pasta if you don't care about fiber)
  • 60g of Preggo Mushroom(or any other sauce. again, if you don't care about fiber even though your colon does)
  • 30g of shredded mozzarella or two slices of mozzarella. Fat free or regular. Up to you
  • Sour cream or fat free plain yogurt
  • (Optional) Half an avocado to top the spaghetti with

Steps

  1. Season ground beef and cook until you brown it(maximum flavor)
  2. Cook the pasta according to the instructions on packaging
  3. Add beef, pasta, sauce into pot and mix well. Then add the cheese and melt it
  4. Grab a heaping spoon of sour cream or yogurt and mix into the pot as well as your avocado
  5. Look at your yummy, pink, gooey spaghetti
  6. Enjoy!
